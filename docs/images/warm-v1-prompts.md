## Ogród — kompozycja

> Create the outdoor garden companion scene to the selected warm pixel-art interior of a dog game. Match the creamy and honey palette, sage greens, teal accents, warm dark brown pixel outlines, crisp high-detail pixel clusters, charming handcrafted props and gentle sunny light. Side-on 2D orthographic garden panorama. At far left include a short slice of the same cream-plaster house with timber trim, a matching exterior door and a small window. Behind the play area: wooden picket fence, foliage and a large tree framing the right. Keep the bottom quarter open and walkable. Add a shaded pergola with plush bed and water bowl, vegetable bed, digging patch, fetching area, rounded bushes, agility marker, puddle, sprinkler and signpost. Space objects apart for the dog sprite. No UI, text, dog, border, blur, anti-aliasing, isometric or 3D.

## Tła bez przedmiotów

> Preserve the exact panorama and pixel-art architecture. Remove all movable
> props and reconstruct continuous wall/floor (home) or grass/fence (garden).
> Keep dark door and window recesses for separate PNG sprites. Do not crop.

## Osobne obiekty

> From the selected reference, faithfully isolate and redraw ONLY the named
> object as one complete side-on orthographic pixel-art game sprite. Match
> warm honey wood, sage and cream materials and crisp detailed pixel clusters.
> Tight framing, genuinely transparent background, no wall, floor, grass,
> characters, UI, text or border.

Z tym szablonem powstały osobne drzwi, okna, pergola, legowiska, miski,
grządka, miejsce kopania, mata aportowania, krzak, przeszkoda, kałuża,
zraszacz, tabliczka, konewka, piłka oraz meble i dekoracje domu.
