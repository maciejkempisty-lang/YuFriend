## Wariant 1 — ciepły dom

> Use case: stylized-concept. Create VARIANT 1, a polished production concept for the interior home level of a warm child-friendly pixel-art dog game. The supplied current home scene is a composition reference only; the supplied puppy sprite sheet is a style and pixel-detail reference only. Draw the room WITHOUT the puppy, without UI, text, icons, or labels. Side-on 2D game background, an extremely wide horizontal 4:1 room panorama, full wall and floor visible, straight orthographic perspective, all furniture entirely inside the canvas. Keep a comparable functional layout: cosy dog bed and feeding area on left, small sage-green kitchen left-center, big window at center with view of a soft green garden, warm floor lamp and low gramophone cabinet right-center, guitar and door on right. Make every object visibly more crafted than the supplied simple background, with intentional 1-pixel highlights, material texture, soft pixel clusters, clear silhouette and dark warm outlines comparable in sophistication to the puppy sprite. Palette: honey wood, cream plaster, sage green, muted teal, golden sunlight. Beautiful, tidy, welcoming children's game scene. Crisp genuine pixel-art edges, no anti-aliasing, no painterly blur, no 3D rendering, no heavy black lines. Frame as one continuous scene, no margins or border. Ensure the upper wall has breathing room and bottom floor occupies roughly one quarter. Reference image 1: current home level layout. Reference image 2: puppy sprite style/detail. No dog in generated room.

Give each prop distinctive material detailing; retain generous walkable wooden floor along bottom quarter and enough negative wall space above. Polished game art, readable silhouettes, beautiful rich pixel craftsmanship; no soft anti-aliasing, no blur, no 3D rendering, no painterly brushwork. Reference image 1: layout only. Reference image 2: pixel-detail benchmark only.
