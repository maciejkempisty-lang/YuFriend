## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one COMPLETE YuFriend garden panorama concept at the game's PRODUCTION WORLD ASPECT RATIO 4:1, intended to be delivered as a 480x120 logical-pixel image with a 1440x360 nearest-neighbor preview. Input 1 is the current YuFriend yard screenshot: reference for the existing fence, grass, palette and exact puppy identity. Input 2 is the established sunny garden concept: reference for the complete set of existing gameplay props, not a scene to copy verbatim. Input 3 is the puppy sprite reference. Create a NEW uninterrupted side-view pixel-art panorama of the same cozy garden, full garden from far left to far right, no UI or phone frame. Preserve the current game's warm sage-green lawn, wooden picket fence, pale blue sky and soft trees. Place ALL existing interactive stations, separated and readable, in this exact left-to-right sequence matching game coordinates: at far left a wooden HOME GATE in the fence; next a shade canopy with woven dog bed and small water bowl; then sniff bush 1; a shallow puddle; a raised wooden flower bed with daisies, red poppies and lavender; a small sandy digging patch with only a subtle buried-treasure hint; sniff bush 2; a spacious fetch lawn with blue ball and two small marker flags; compact obstacle-course props including cones, low jump and stay platform; sniff bush 3; a garden sprinkler with hose; and at far right a discreet outing/potty sign by the fence. Place the SAME golden retriever puppy Gloria at playable scale around the center-left, golden fur, caramel floppy ears, cream muzzle and paws, head tuft, dark eyes, facing right. A tiny butterfly, bird, squirrel, snail and visiting cat can appear behind or near the fence, secondary to the gameplay stations. Make every object fully visible, with clear traversable grass strip in foreground and room for puppy motion. Crisp coarse pixel art on a coherent grid, no blur or gradients, simple outlines and limited warm palette. CRITICAL FORMAT: fit the WHOLE designed world into a very wide thin 4:1 rectangular art strip, NOT a 3:1 scene; if the generated canvas is taller, leave only genuine transparent empty padding above and below the complete 4:1 panorama, so the full strip can be extracted without cutting any visible element. No text, labels, icons, HUD, extra stations, buildings, watermark or borders.
